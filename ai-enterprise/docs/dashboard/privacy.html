Privacy Policy

AI Eco Systems · Effective date: 4 November 2026

Who we are

AI Eco Systems is a small, private software project run by its two founders. It sells original print-on-demand designs in our own Etsy shop and promotes them on our own Pinterest and Instagram accounts. The source code is public at github.com/robin100a541-prog/AI-Eco-systems.

Whose data we access

The app is used only by the founders, with their own accounts. It does not offer a service to other Pinterest, Instagram or Etsy users, and it never asks other people to log in.

What we access and why

  • Pinterest: our own boards and Pins, to publish Pins that link to our own Etsy listings. Trending keywords are used to decide which designs to make. They are used at the moment they are read and are not stored.
  • Instagram: our own professional account, to publish our own product images.
  • Etsy: our own shop and listings, and public listing data, to plan and publish our own products.

What we store

  • The access tokens for our own accounts, in a file on the founder's own computer (outside the source code, readable only by that user) or as an encrypted secret in our private GitHub repository settings. Tokens are never published, logged or shared.
  • A log of what the app published (time, link and the id that the platform returned), so that daily limits are respected.
  • We do not store data about other users, and we do not store data from the Pinterest API except what is needed to manage our own Pins.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone.
  • We do not use data for advertising targeting or profiling.
  • We do not collect passwords. Logging in always happens on the platform's own page (OAuth).

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when we disconnect an account or stop using the app. Revoking the app's access in your Pinterest, Instagram or Etsy settings stops all access immediately.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/robin100a541-prog/AI-Eco-systems/issues.

We will update this page if anything changes and change the effective date above.
