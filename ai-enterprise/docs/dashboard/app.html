About the AI Eco Systems app

A single-business app for the founders' own accounts. No other users.

What it does

We design original typographic and pattern artwork, produce it through Printify (print on demand) and sell it in our own Etsy shop. The app helps us with three tasks:

  1. Plan: reads Pinterest's trending keywords (GET /v5/trends/keywords/{region}/top/{trend_type}) to choose seasonal themes, for example winter, holidays and gifts. The keywords are used directly and not stored.
  2. Publish: creates a Pin (POST /v5/pins) for each new product, with our own product image and a link to our own Etsy listing.
  3. Limit: publishes at most 10 Pins per day, logs every Pin, and can be stopped at any time with an emergency stop.

How users authenticate

The only users are the two founders. A founder runs python -m aie connect pinterest. The app opens Pinterest's own OAuth page, the founder logs in and approves there, and Pinterest redirects back to a local address on the founder's computer. The app exchanges the code for a token and stores it outside the source code, readable only by that user. The app never sees or stores a password.

Scopes requested

  • user_accounts:read: identify our own account
  • boards:read: choose which of our boards a Pin goes to
  • pins:read, pins:write: create and check our own Pins

Content rules

Every design is our own. A check in the code stops trademarks, characters, famous people and copied motifs before anything is published. Product listings state that AI tools were used to help create the design.
