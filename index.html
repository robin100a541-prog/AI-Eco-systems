About AI ECO

AI ECO is a small independent business from Skövde, Sweden. We sell original printed gift products, such as t-shirts and mugs, through our own Etsy shop. Our designs include gifts for dads, moms, creators, realtors and teachers, Christmas gifts, and Swedish- and Arabic-themed gifts.

About the app: AI Eco Systems Publisher

  • An internal tool used only by AI ECO.
  • Creates boards and publishes Pins of AI ECO's own products (image, title, description and a link to the product page) to AI ECO's own Pinterest business account, on a schedule.
  • Does not access, collect or store data from other Pinterest users.
  • Pinterest API scopes used: boards:read, boards:write, pins:read, pins:write, user_accounts:read.
